🚨 New paper on arXiv: [Logarithmic negativity typically equals exact entanglement cost](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.01320) w/ Bowen Ouyang

For random induced mixed states, we show that the logarithmic negativity — an efficiently computable entanglement measure — generically coincides with the exact entanglement cost under PPT-preserving operations, giving it a clean operational meaning.
