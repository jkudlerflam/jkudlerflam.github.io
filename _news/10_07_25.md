🚨 New paper on arXiv: [Emergent Mixed States for Baby Universes and Black Holes](https://arxiv.org/abs/2510.06376) w/ Edward Witten.

We explored the fate of quantum states in the semiclassical limit of the AdS/CFT correspondence, focusing on cases where the gravitational side involves black holes or “baby universes." This resolved an [interesting puzzle](https://arxiv.org/abs/2408.02720) posed by Stefano Antonini and Pratik Rath on the nature of closed universes in AdS/CFT.
