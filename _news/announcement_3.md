Geoff Penington and I have been [awarded second prize](https://www.gravityresearchfoundation.org/announcement) in the 2025 Gravity Research Foundation Essay Contest for [our paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2504.01058) on quantum teleportation into black holes! Our work explored the deep relationship between information and energy in quantum black holes.
